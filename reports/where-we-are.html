One blocker sits under almost everything left

Reviewing calls. Comparing what operators say against what the training teaches. Better forecasting. Coaching notes that point at real calls. All of it needs the words from a call, and right now we cannot get them.

The transcripts exist — 1,158,255 of them, with speaker separation. But the column that links a transcript to its call (callLogs.deepgramTranscriptId) is empty on all 290,705 recent calls. So we can see transcripts and we can see calls, and we cannot tell which belongs to which.

Until that's solved, the op reports are the substitute — a human writing down what went wrong and what should have happened. That works, and it's what everything built so far runs on.

Things I am deliberately not doing

  • No grading calls. Not his job, and the company's AI QA flags 93.4% of calls positive — a detector that says yes to nearly everything tells you nothing.
  • Nothing about payment audio. The system mutes it. Silence there is never treated as a finding.
  • No mixing with marketing. Separate repo, separate sheets, separate trigger variable. Only Railway and the database are shared.
  • No guessing a number to fill a blank. Where our figure and theirs disagree, the sheet says so instead of quietly printing a different one.
